[Retail eShop](https://get.dxfeed.com) is a B2C solution that provides end users with a full life-cycle platform that includes shopping cart, payment system, subscription management, personal profiles, and support.

The system is represented as two independent services:

1. Subscription platform (dxFeed Retail) responsible for subscription management, entitlement control, and reporting.
2. dxFeed endpoint providing data feeds. The scheme below briefly describes the overall process of using the dxFeed Retail eShop service to deliver market data to end users via a partner's application.
