## Key features

The features list includes, but is not limited to:

- Personal data protection (GDPR compliant)
- Subscription management
- Exchanges agreements acceptance
- Email notifications
- Credentials management
- Recurring and one-time payments processing
- Online support

## Compliance

We are committed to simplifying compliance processes for our partners and clients. As a part of our dedication, we have incorporated the following features:

1. Reporting: A special service takes care of sending reports to exchanges in predefined formats and ensuring compliance with individual exchange requirements so that our clients rest assured that every report meets the specific standards of each exchange eliminating the need to navigate complex regulatory nuances.
2. Tokenized access: Our system offers secure tokenized access to real-time data via short-term personalized API keys. This enhances the integrity of market data and ensures that access is exclusively granted to users who have undergone rigorous verification process.
3. Subscriber status verification: Through a diligently performed data clarification process, we verify professional users who may incorrectly identify themselves as non-professional. It ensures compliance with the product usage rules.
4. Blocking mechanism: Our blocking functionality guarantees that violating users are promptly restricted from gaining access to market data.
5. Blacklist: A comprehensive blacklisting mechanism prevents any potential violating users from creating new accounts or continuing to use market data.

We retain the authority to respond to requests from market exchanges and initiate necessary actions that may affect certain users.
