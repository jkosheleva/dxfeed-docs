## How to submit a ticket

1. Log in on the [Help Desk](https://portal.dxfeed.com).
2. Choose a topic.
3. Fill up all fields. See the [Ticket Requirements](/support/help-desk/ticket-requirements/#ticket-requirements) page for details.
4. Click the **Create** button.

:::note
To submit a support ticket, you must be an authorized customer with the necessary credentials to access our customer portal. If you haven't received your login details, please reach out to your sales manager.
:::

## Ticket priority

[Help Desk](https://portal.dxfeed.com) has 4 Priority levels:

- ASAP
- Critical
- Urgent
- Normal

[Technical Support Procedures](/support/technical-support-procedures/#technical-support-procedures) page describes how we understand these priorities.

## Ticket types

| **Priority level** | **Description** | **Affect** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Service Call | Service request (regular situation) | No |
| Incident | Contains a specific problem | Financial or recreational |
