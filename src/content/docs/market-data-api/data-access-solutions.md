## Overview

Our programming language APIs include ready-made solutions and are accompanied by such access solutions as WebSocket, REST, and FIX.

Our proprietary technology powers our entire API, ensuring high performance and low latency in market data. We provide access to essential information and offer models that enable the creation of specialized entities, ensuring that this information can be tailored to various needs. Utilizing our key technologies, we manage and process this data, aligning it with your specific requirements and high standards.

### Ready-made solutions

Our ready-made solutions are pre-built libraries and tools for quick integration into applications:

- [Multiplatform API](/market-data-api/data-access-solutions/multiplatform-api/#multiplatform-api) (Java, C++, .Net, Swift, Go)
- [JavaScript API](/market-data-api/data-access-solutions/javascript-api/#javascript-api)
